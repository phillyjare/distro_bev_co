01 — Executive Overview

A Visionary Nonprofit
for Kansas City

The Kansas City Botanical Garden is a visionary nonprofit initiative dedicated to transforming urban space into a thriving hub of natural beauty, community engagement, and environmental education. Rooted in our mission to enrich the lives of Kansas City residents, the garden will serve as a dynamic destination offering community events, hands-on educational programming, and sustainable urban beautification.

Designed as a self-sustaining nonprofit, the project includes a revenue-generating model that leases on-site space to service-oriented businesses aligned with our mission—such as cafés, wellness studios, or floral shops. These partnerships will help fund operations while enhancing the visitor experience.

By combining public benefit with smart sustainability, the Kansas City Botanical Garden will become a cornerstone of culture, conservation, and community connection for generations to come.

03 — Operating Model

Operating
Model

The Kansas City Botanical Garden will operate under a 501(c)(3) nonprofit structure, responsible for the full management and stewardship of the garden. This includes all operating expenses such as labor, programming, capital improvements, facilities maintenance, and administrative overhead.

Hybrid Funding Model

The garden will be funded through a combination of public grants, private donations, and a mission-aligned earned revenue strategy. A key element includes the development of commercial spaces within the garden, leased to carefully selected, service-oriented for-profit businesses.

Tenant Revenue Structure

Commercial tenants will operate under hybrid lease agreements that combine a base rent guarantee with a revenue-sharing component, creating a steady and scalable income stream to support the nonprofit’s operational sustainability and reduce reliance on donations.

Long-Term Sustainability

By integrating earned income with traditional nonprofit funding, the operating model ensures long-term financial health while preserving the garden as a public resource and cultural landmark for all Kansas Citians.

06 — Impact

Community Impact
& Future
Development

Community Impact

  • Promotes inclusive wellness and mental health
  • Serves as an educational hub for sustainability
  • Builds local partnerships and boosts eco-tourism
  • Offers employment and volunteer opportunities
  • Provides a peaceful and restorative place for all members of the community
